# Code of Conduct

Gene OS is maintained as an open, academic-friendly project. Everyone who participates, whether in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space, is expected to help keep it welcoming.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful and patient. People contribute in different languages and at different experience levels.
- Give constructive, specific feedback on code and science, and accept it the same way.
- Credit others' work, data and publications accurately.
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification before assuming the worst.

## Unacceptable behaviour
- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Plagiarism or misrepresenting someone else's results as your own.

## Enforcement
The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and temporarily or permanently block participants who don't follow this code. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer on Telegram at **@Bitologist** or through [10ck.ir](https://10ck.ir). Reports are handled confidentially.
